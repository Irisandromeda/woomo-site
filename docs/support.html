Support

Questions, a bug, or an idea? Write to irisandromeda.dev@gmail.com or use Profile → Support → Contact us in the app. We usually reply within 2 business days, in English, Ukrainian or Russian.

When reporting a problem, please include your device model, iOS version and the app version (shown at the bottom of the Profile screen).

Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my WooMo Plus subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → WooMo on your iPhone or iPad and tap Cancel Subscription, or go to Profile → Subscription → Manage in App Store in WooMo. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. You keep Plus until the end of the paid period. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I get a refund?

All payments go through Apple, so we cannot issue refunds ourselves. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I changed my phone. How do I restore my data and subscription?

Sign in with the same Apple or Google account you used before: your diary syncs automatically. To restore the subscription, tap Profile → Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID.

Do I need to create an account?

You can try WooMo without signing in. To keep your data safe and use it on several devices, save your plan with Sign in with Apple or Google.

WooMo logged the wrong food or portion. What do I do?

AI estimates can be wrong. Tap the entry in your diary to change the food, grams or meal, or just tell WooMo in the chat: “it was 150 g, not 250”.

A product is missing or has wrong nutrition values.

Scan the barcode and add the values from the label, or send us the barcode and a photo of the label and we will fix it.

How do I export my data?

Open Profile → Account and data → Export data, choose the period and format (CSV or JSON) and share the file.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open Profile → Account and data → Delete account. All your data is deleted from our servers immediately; encrypted backups are overwritten within 6 months. If you can't open the app, email us from the address linked to your account. See the Privacy Policy.

Does WooMo work with Apple Health?

Yes, if you allow it. You can change permissions at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → WooMo.
