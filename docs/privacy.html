Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data the WooMo app (iPhone and iPad) collects, why, who processes it, how long it is kept and what rights you have. We wrote it to be read, not to hide things in fine print.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Olha Dyka, an individual developer based in Ukraine, who publishes WooMo on the App Store. Contact for any privacy question or request: irisandromeda.dev@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

  • Account data. An internal user ID. If you sign in with Apple or Google, the email address and name that the provider shares with us (with Apple you can hide your email). Signing in with Apple or Google is required to use WooMo. While you answer the first setup questions, before signing in, your answers are kept in a temporary account tied only to that ID and are moved to your account when you sign in.
  • Profile and goals. Sex, date of birth, height, current and target weight, activity level, goal, calorie and nutrient targets, units, language and time zone. You enter these during onboarding or in settings.
  • Health and nutrition data. Your food diary (meals, foods, portions, calories and nutrients), water intake, weight and body measurements, optional progress photos, and the foods and recipes you create. This is health data and is treated as a special category of data (see section 4).
  • Photos you send. Photos of meals you send to WooMo for analysis, and optional progress photos.
  • Chat with WooMo. The messages you exchange with the AI assistant, and short notes the assistant keeps to personalise its answers (for example, “usually drinks coffee without sugar”). You can view and delete these notes.
  • Apple Health. Only if you allow it, the app reads or writes data such as weight and energy in Apple Health. Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising or sold, and is not shared with anyone except the processors listed below that are needed to provide the feature.
  • Device and technical data. Push notification token, app version, device language and time zone, consent records (what you agreed to and when), and technical logs needed for security and debugging (for example, IP address in server logs).
  • AI usage records. How many AI requests your account made and their cost, used to apply fair-use limits.
  • Purchases. When subscriptions are available, Apple processes the payment. We receive only the subscription status, never your card details.

We do not use advertising SDKs, do not track you across other apps or websites, and do not sell or rent personal data.

3. Why we use it and on what legal basis

  • To provide the app (account, diary, calorie targets, progress, sync between your devices): performance of our contract with you.
  • To process health data (diary, weight, measurements, photos): your explicit consent, which you give in the app and can withdraw at any time.
  • To run the AI assistant (analysing your messages and meal photos and logging food for you): your explicit consent to AI processing.
  • Security, fraud and abuse prevention, fair-use limits, backups: our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe and available.
  • Legal obligations, such as answering lawful requests from authorities and keeping records required by tax or consumer law.

4. Health data

Your food diary, weight, body measurements and photos reveal information about your health. Under the GDPR (Article 9) and Ukrainian law, we process this data only with your explicit consent and only to provide the features you use. We do not use health data for advertising, profiling for third parties or any purpose unrelated to the app. If you withdraw consent, we stop processing it; you can delete it at any time (section 8).

WooMo is a nutrition tracker, not a medical device. Its estimates and AI answers are not medical advice.

Food nutrition values come from public databases: USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts. When you scan a barcode, only the barcode number is looked up; no personal data is sent to these databases.

We may disclose data if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users, and only to the extent required.

6. International transfers

Your data is stored in the EU. Some providers (Anthropic, Apple, Google, RevenueCat, and Supabase as a US company) may process it in the United States. These transfers are protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and, where applicable, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, together with encryption in transit.

7. How long we keep data

  • Your account and diary: for as long as you keep the account.
  • Items you delete in the app (entries, foods, photos): removed permanently after 30 days.
  • Chat messages: 365 days, then deleted automatically.
  • AI usage records: 400 days, for limits and accounting.
  • Unfinished setups (temporary accounts where sign-in was never completed) inactive for 90 days: deleted with all their data.
  • When you delete your account: all your data is deleted from the live database immediately.
  • Encrypted backups: kept for up to 6 months for disaster recovery, then overwritten. Deleted data is not restored from backups except to recover from a failure, and in that case we delete it again.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR, UK GDPR and Ukrainian law) you have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy of it: use Profile → Account and data → Export data in the app (CSV or JSON);
  • correct it: edit your profile, goals and diary in the app;
  • delete it: use Profile → Account and data → Delete account, or write to us;
  • withdraw consent to health data or AI processing at any time, without affecting processing done before;
  • object to or restrict processing, and data portability;
  • complain to a data protection authority, for example the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights or the authority in your EU country of residence.

To exercise any right, write to irisandromeda.dev@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Each user can access only their own data, enforced by row-level security in the database. Access to production systems is limited and protected with strong authentication. Backups are encrypted with a key kept offline.

10. Children

WooMo is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For significant changes we will notify you in the app before they take effect.
